Our Plastering & Ceiling Services

We shape spaces by delivering smooth, crack-resistant, and aesthetically finished walls and ceilings.

Gypsum Plastering

Expert application of stucco gypsum plaster for smooth, crack-resistant, and aesthetically finished walls and ceilings — ideal for residential and commercial spaces.

False Ceiling Works

Precision gypsum board framing and suspended grid ceiling installations. Integrates profile lighting coves, tracks, and thermal insulation.

POP Design Works

Handcrafted dome elements, modern step cornices, ceiling light coves, and custom plaster of Paris moldings for luxurious spaces.

Wall Partition

Acoustically insulated dry wall gypsum partition partitions utilizing metal GI framing. Ideal for executive cabins and rooms.

Cornice & Moulding

Stylized ornamental borders, Victorian scroll patterns, and minimalist steps that join ceilings and walls seamlessly.

Interior & Exterior Plastering

Direct blocks application for interiors and cement-based plaster coatings for exterior structures matching modern alignments.

Commercial & Residential

End-to-end gypsum contract setups for luxury villas, corporate offices, hotels, hospitals, and high-rise apartments.

All Finishing Works

Surface sanding, joint reinforcing fiber meshes, edge beadings, and final level verification handovers.

Gypsum plastering provides a perfectly level surface that is white and ready for painting, which completely eliminates the need for double-coat wall putty. It does not require water curing, meaning your walls are dry in 3 days rather than the 21 days required for cement.
