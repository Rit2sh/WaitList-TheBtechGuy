About TheBTechGuy

What is TheBTechGuy?

TheBTechGuy is India's Builder Network. We are building one platform where ambitious people can transform ideas into real companies. Whether you're a student building your first project, a founder launching a startup, a developer looking for meaningful work, or an investor searching for promising founders, TheBTechGuy brings everyone together.

One Platform. Endless Opportunities.
Why We Exist

Consolidating the Fragmented Stack

The Problem

Today's builders waste critical building hours switching between fragmented platforms:

  • LinkedIn for networking & recruiting
  • GitHub for showcasing developer profiles
  • Product Hunt for launching MVP products
  • Wellfound for early-stage startup jobs
  • Devfolio for exploring hackathons
  • Discord & Telegram for fragmented chat channels

Our Solution

TheBTechGuy combines everything builders need into one single, powerful ecosystem:

  • One Unified Profile matching developers & startups
  • Startup Launches to get immediate feedback
  • Find Cofounders, marketers, & developers
  • Interactive Hackathons & building sprints
  • Direct Seed Funding, grants & investor networking
  • Integrated AI Tools for pitch deck reviews
